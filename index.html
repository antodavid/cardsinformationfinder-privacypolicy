Privacy Policy

Cards Information Finder (Android app) · Effective date: October 31, 2026

In short: the app looks up public information about a card's BIN (the first 6 digits). We never ask for, collect or store a full card number, CVV, PIN or your name. When you use Tap & Check (NFC), the card is read on your phone only; nothing from the card is saved or sent, except the 6-digit BIN when you choose to look it up. The app is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob.

This policy explains what information the Cards Information Finder app ("the app", "we", "us") processes, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

1. Information we process

BIN numbers you look up

When you check a BIN, the 6 digits you entered are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our lookup service hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS). The returned BIN details (such as card network, issuing bank, card type and country) are public reference data, not personal data. BIN details may be added to a shared "Top searched BINs" list stored in Google Firebase. This list contains BIN information only, with no information about who searched it.

Saved BINs

If you save a BIN, it is stored in Google Firebase together with a random identifier generated by the app on your device. This identifier is not linked to your name, email address or phone number. You can delete all saved BINs at any time from Settings → Clear saved BINs.

Tap & Check (NFC card reading)

If your phone supports NFC, you can hold a contactless payment card to it to read the same public data a payment terminal sees: the card number, expiry date and card application name. This data:

  • is processed only in your phone's memory and is discarded when you leave the screen;
  • is never stored, logged, uploaded or shared;
  • is shown masked on screen (only the first 6 and last 4 digits are visible), and screenshots of that screen are blocked;
  • does not include the CVV/security code, PIN, cardholder name or transaction history, which the app does not read.

Only if you tap Look up BIN details are the first 6 digits (the BIN) sent for a normal BIN lookup, as described above. No payment or transaction is ever made.

AI Summary

If you request an AI Summary, the BIN is sent to our service on AWS, which uses an AI model (Amazon Bedrock) to write a description of that BIN. Summaries may be cached by BIN so they load faster next time. No personal information is sent or stored.

Notifications

To send you notifications, the app stores a Firebase Cloud Messaging token (a random identifier of the app install) in Google Firebase. The app may also schedule occasional reminders on your device. You can turn off notifications at any time in your phone's settings.

App usage and crash data

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app's features are used (for example, "BIN searched" or "screen opened") and Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports. This includes device model, OS version, app version, approximate region and an app-instance identifier. It never includes card data.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob (banner, native, rewarded and app-open ads). AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and ad interactions to show, measure and (where you allow it) personalise ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Settings stored on your device

Preferences such as your theme (System, Light or Dark) and simple counters (for example, how many searches you have made) are stored only on your device.

2. What we do not collect

  • Full card numbers, CVV/security codes, PINs or expiry dates (NFC data stays on your device and is discarded)
  • Your name, email address, phone number or account login (the app has no accounts)
  • Contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or precise location

We do not sell your information. Information is processed by these service providers only to run the app:

  • Google: Firebase (database, analytics, crash reports, notifications) and AdMob (advertising). Google Privacy Policy
  • Amazon Web Services: BIN lookup and AI Summary services (United States). AWS Privacy Notice

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users or the app.

5. Where data is processed

Our service providers may process data in the United States and other countries, which may have different data-protection laws from your country. They use appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Saved BINs are kept until you clear them in Settings.
  • NFC card data is never retained.
  • Analytics and crash data are kept according to Google Firebase retention settings.
  • On-device settings are removed when you uninstall the app.

To ask us to delete data linked to your app install, email us at the address below.

7. Security

All network traffic uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Card data read through NFC never leaves your device's memory. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we design the app to collect as little as possible.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your information, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us. You can also change your ad consent choices by clearing the app's data or reinstalling the app.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.
